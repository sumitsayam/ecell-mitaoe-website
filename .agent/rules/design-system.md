# Design System Rules — E-Cell MITAOE

/* Dark Surfaces (Spiral Opening & Final Returning Spiral) */
--color-dark: #0B1020;

--color-dark-text: #F5F8FF;

/* Architectural Blueprint Grid */

## 2. Typography Standards
- **Display Headings:** `Sora` (`font-display`) — Heavy, confident, tight tracking.
- **Body & UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`font-sans`) — High-legibility neutral sans-serif.
- **Technical & Numbers:** `IBM Plex Mono` (`font-mono`) — Mechanical monospace for coordinates, numbering (001–010), timestamps, and circular text.

## 3. Visual Language
- Minimal, editorial, architectural, high-contrast.
- Avoid generic SaaS cards, excessive drop shadows, or generic AI gradients.
- White and pale blue surfaces dominate editorial storytelling; pitch dark navy frames the kinetic spirals.
